Service Assurance

How Anchor solves inconsistency

The Anchor Standard is backed by a system. A clean home should not depend on who happened to show up that day.

Anchor’s service system gives every visit a defined scope, gives cleaners a clear way to document completion and exceptions, and gives you visibility when something needs attention.

The result is recurring home cleaning that is easier to manage, easier to correct, and far more accountable.

  • Defined service scope

    Every home has a clear, documented cleaning scope so expectations do not live in someone’s memory.

  • Cleaner accountability

    Cleaners work from visit-specific checklists and can flag exceptions, notes, and issues as they happen.

  • Visible issue resolution

    You can report concerns, see when they’ve been acknowledged, and follow the issue through resolution.
