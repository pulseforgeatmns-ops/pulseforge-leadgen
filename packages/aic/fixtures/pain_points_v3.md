# Pain Points v3

## Mission
Transform founder-led businesses into business-machine businesses.

## Transformation
Current: I do everything myself.
Future: My business operates through systems and managers.

## ICP
Founder-led businesses with traction that still run through the founder — agencies, trades, professional services, operator-owned firms.
Signals: founder-led, owner-operated, I do everything myself

## Disqualifiers
PE-backed, professionally governed, already systemized with managers.

## Pain: Founder Dependency
The business cannot operate without the founder in the daily loop.
Supported by: Hiring
Observed through: Job Posts, owner operational, recruiting, burnout language
Buying signals: hiring, expansion, owner operational

## Pain: Hiring
The founder is stuck recruiting because there is no management layer.
Observed through: Job Posts, we are hiring, now hiring

## Confidence
Job postings and owner-operational language increase certainty of founder dependency.
Unknown: geography and revenue bands are not named in this document.
