Direct Mail Follow-Up Opening

Hey, I'm Mike with Anchor Cleaning. We recently sent some info over about commercial cleaning. I'm just stopping by to make sure it got to the right person. Who usually handles cleaning or facility vendors here?

Use this for Campaign 001 queue items — then tap the task in Queue and log the visit with Max.

Opening

Hey, I'm with Anchor Cleaning. We're local and help businesses keep their spaces consistently clean. Quick question: who usually handles your cleaning or facilities vendors?

Qualifying Questions

  • Are you using an outside cleaner now?
  • How often is the space cleaned?
  • Any issues with consistency, communication, quality, or scheduling?
  • Who usually handles vendor decisions?

Walkthrough Ask

Would it be worth having Jake stop by for a quick walkthrough and give you a second option?

Common Objections

"We already have someone."
Totally. A lot of the businesses we talk to already have a cleaner. Is the current service consistent?
"Not interested."
No problem. Is there a better person to leave this with in case cleaning ever becomes an issue?
"Send info."
Absolutely. Who should I send it to, and would a quick walkthrough be useful or too early?
"We're all set."
Glad to hear it. If anything changes, who would be the best person to check back with?

What To Listen For

  • Inconsistent cleaner
  • Missed details
  • Poor communication
  • Complaints from staff/customers
  • No clear schedule
  • Price shopping
  • Multiple locations
  • Upcoming change, renovation, new tenant, or new manager

What Not To Do

  • Don't quote prices
  • Don't promise availability
  • Don't criticize their current cleaner
  • Don't push if they clearly say no
  • Don't guess on insurance or contracts
  • Route serious questions to Jake
