Look up or close an exact grant

Read a durable receipt after installation, a missing response, or a later login. This does not re-approve anything.

Grant receipts describe historical committed transitions, not current review state, buyer readiness, capacity, or preparation availability. A validated draft result is a current persisted snapshot, not evidence that a message was sent. Consumption stays charged; there is no retry or refund.

Prepare drafts — a separate decision

One explicit attempt creates canonical draft artifacts for the exact installed batch. It can permanently consume the operation before finishing. Failures, reloads and revocation do not authorize another attempt, refund, replacement batch, or larger window.

Close the exact grant

Revocation closes only this grant. It preserves recorded reviews, consumption, drafts and history, and does not alter activation settings or clear a preparation-attempt fence.
