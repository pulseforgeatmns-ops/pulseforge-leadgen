A QUICK GUIDE

The numbers, in plain English.

Still holding
What the tokens left in your wallet are worth now.
Money back
What you received from selling. This includes your original money, not just profit.
Total profit / loss
Profit from sales plus the change in value of what you still hold.
Original money at risk
Money put in that your sales have not yet paid back. Tokens you still hold can lose value.
