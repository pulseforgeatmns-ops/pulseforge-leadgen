# Tenant-10 Anchor recovery: approval proposal

Status: local preparation and tests only. No publication, deployment, production schema/data writes, provider research, message preparation or sends are approved by this document. All example approval references, identities, times and activation IDs must remain unset until an explicit decision.

This is the current SPEC-262 proposal. It supersedes the unpublished broad-gate SPEC-261 operator plan for rollout. Do not deploy or roll back to the frozen broad-gate variant: it pauses other tenants. The immutable historical patch remains evidence, not the rollout recommendation.

## What can resume
Only the existing, explicitly approved tenant-10 program and an explicitly reviewed finite identity batch. Identities may span fresh daily envelopes/refills within the original program window and stricter daily/total caps. Every dispatch still needs frozen copy, current canonical execution approval, sender/inbox binding, actual payload binding, existing eligibility checks, quarantine and an irreversible one-item handoff marker.

Preparation, deterministic template drafting and delegated canonical approval each require explicit operations in the preparation scope. Email permission does not authorize preparation. This release rejects enabled paid research and paid copy/enrichment/LLM preparation. Existing approved ready artifacts can be reused; deterministic existing-template refill is a separate named permission. If current artifacts do not exist, this patch does not manufacture authority to create them.

The latest approved read found no current-day prepared envelope/preparation row. The source mission is discover/Discovering with no matching Max/Paige/Emmett artifacts. This verifies no current ready batch in those sources, not an empty CRM. A separately approved, bounded existing-candidate draft operation must first produce current canonical artifacts; the local source does not authorize paid discovery or reuse expired copy. Candidate quality/count remains subject to the bounded readiness export. The source program's existing expiry and caps are hard outer limits; this recovery does not extend them.

## Tenant and channel boundaries
Non-Anchor email, SMS, calls, research, preparation and Max follow their original query/lock/control branches without a recovery-table dependency. No new permission is granted to them. Calls and SMS, including tenant10, retain prior semantics; “sms: closed” means this grant provides no SMS authority. Current production's missing call-only column still makes its original SMS query fail closed; this patch does not add that column.

Tenant-10 manual CRM email and unsupported transports stay closed while recovery is installed. Every actual tenant-10 CRM email payload receives durable recipient/contact/company quarantine checks. Brevo uses its existing managed route and canonical sender/inbox, with per-command credential overrides rejected. No SMTP identity or secret belongs in the manifest.

Quarantine is nonexpiring and never automatically released. It preserves the original UNKNOWN attempt, exact microsecond token, reason and canonical execution. Canonical execution prospect_id is the governed candidate/company identity; item prospect_id is the CRM contact. Company/candidate quarantine deliberately holds other people at that company and conservative aliases; duplicate email under a new CRM row is also held. No override or future release workflow is implemented.

One user approval may cover the exact reviewed recipient/copy/sender plus its canonical execution-approval binding and bounded activation/dispatch; technical checks remain separate and must not self-create approval. Delegated existing-program copy approval is an additional explicit policy choice, never inferred.

## Rollback and expiry
Clear/revoke the durable tenant10 scoped grant while retaining this narrow gate-aware build, quarantine and permanent counters. Tenant10 closes; other tenants continue their original branches. Expiry/removal also closes tenant10 permissions. Do not clear the immutable maintenance latch, delete counters/quarantine, drop tables, or roll back to pre-gate or broad-gate code. A forward fix or separately reviewed narrow closed build is the safe binary fallback.

## Remaining limits
The nullable/no-default schema intentionally fails the historical spreadsheet rollout preflight requiring NOT NULL/default false. It is not a claim that the old spreadsheet rollout is ready. Existing explicit values/default/nullability in other environments are preserved.

Final leaf checks reuse current suppression/ownership predicates and locked contact/company snapshots. They reject completed post-claim ownership/reply/suppression changes. Legacy absent-row AO/touchpoint writers do not all participate in the recovery lock, so this is not a new global serialization guarantee for every concurrent legacy writer. No wider writer/trigger redesign is included.

Exact publication/deployment revision; shared-DDL approval reference; closure verification; exact historical quarantine decision; current operator ID/role; exact existing program/policy/source/scope hashes; canonical Brevo sender/name/domain/inbox; current approved CRM identities/company aliases/provenance fingerprints; optional explicit NULL initialization list/reference; valid-from/expiry; daily/total email caps; independent preparation operation/item caps; existing-template/canonical-approval delegation choice; unique activation ID; execution approval reference. Keep clients.autosend_enabled=false: the existing governed contract requires legacy autosend disabled and rejects true. Do not turn on legacy autosend as a recovery step.
