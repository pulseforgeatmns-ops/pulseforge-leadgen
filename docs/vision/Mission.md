# Mission

Pulseforge exists to give local service businesses an **intelligent operating partner**—not another disconnected SaaS dashboard.

We turn fragmented commercial activity (leads, emails, calls, inquiries, bookings, reviews, content) into **durable memory**, **explainable recommendations**, and **approved action** so owners and setters spend time on relationships, not on stitching tools together.

## Mission statement

> Help local operators win more of the right work by remembering every meaningful signal and recommending the next best move—always under human authority.

## Who we serve

- **Pulseforge operators** running multi-market lead-gen and setter/closer pipelines
- **Client businesses** (e.g. MSHI, Anchor Cleaning, Pulseforge Nashville) who need scoped intake, outreach, and follow-up
- **Setters and closers** who need trustworthy queues, not noisy CRM clutter

## What success looks like

- A new teammate (or AI session) understands the product from the repo in under 20 minutes
- Every material recommendation can be explained from stored evidence
- Customer-visible actions are intentional, approved, and attributable
- Pipeline truth stays client-scoped and auditable
